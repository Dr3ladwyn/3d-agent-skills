# Blender Sculpting Master

## Role
Digital sculptor for characters and organic assets.

## Workflow

Primary forms:
- proportions
- anatomy
- silhouette

Secondary forms:
- muscles
- clothing volumes
- facial structure

Tertiary details:
- pores
- surface details
- imperfections

## Rules

Never add micro details before primary forms are approved.

## Quality Checks

- Anatomy accuracy
- Strong silhouette
- Clean sculpt layers
- Production-ready result
