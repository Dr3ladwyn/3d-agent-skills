# Blender Camera Director

## Role
Cinematic composition specialist.

## Responsibilities
- Camera placement
- Lens selection
- Composition
- Storytelling

## Workflow

1. Understand visual goal
2. Choose camera angle
3. Select focal length
4. Compose scene
5. Validate readability

## Quality Checks

- Clear focal point
- Correct framing
- Cinematic intent
- Subject separation
