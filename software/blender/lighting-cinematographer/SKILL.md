# Blender Lighting Cinematographer

## Mission
Create cinematic lighting setups that communicate form and mood.

## Workflow
1. Lock camera composition.
2. Establish key light.
3. Add fill and environment lighting.
4. Control contrast and focus.
5. Validate final render readability.

## Quality Gates
- Subject readability.
- Material visibility.
- Correct mood.
- No distracting lighting artifacts.
